# Beejal — 3 minute pitch script

Read it top to bottom. Short sentences on purpose. Say them slowly.
**Bold in brackets** is what you DO, not what you say.

**Before you go up:** have the tool already open on screen, showing London.

416 words. At a slow, clear pace that is about 2 minutes 40, leaving roughly 20 seconds for
pauses and the live click. If you are running long, the honesty section is the only part you
may shorten — never the six boroughs.

---

**[STAND. LOOK UP. WAIT ONE SECOND.]**

I'm Beejal. I worked alone today. This is **Homes vs Hotels**.

---

**[0:08 — slowly]**

Tonight, **one hundred thousand, nine hundred and thirty** children in London are living in
temporary accommodation. Hotels. Hostels. Not homes.

That is the highest number ever recorded.

Most people still say eighty-five thousand. London passed one hundred thousand, and nobody
announced it.

---

**[0:30 — SCROLL TO THE RED AND GREEN CHART. POINT.]**

It is also a money crisis.

Last year boroughs spent **seven hundred and forty million pounds** on temporary accommodation.
Three years ago, two hundred and forty three million. It tripled.

Red is temporary accommodation. Green is prevention — the money that stops the next family
needing help. Red runs away. Green is flat.

It now takes **seventy-one percent** of London's homelessness budget.

These are not my estimates. This is what councils reported to government.

---

**[1:05]**

In five years London approved **three hundred and twenty thousand** homes, and finished one
hundred and eighty four thousand.

**One hundred and twelve thousand were never even started.** Not delayed. Never started.

---

**[1:25 — SCROLL TO THE SCATTER PLOT. This is the important part.]**

Here is where I was wrong.

I assumed the boroughs with the worst crisis would be the ones failing to build. I tested all
thirty-one boroughs. There is **no relationship at all**.

**[POINT AT BARNET, THEN GREENWICH]**

Barnet has worse than average pressure, and still started more homes than it approved.
Greenwich has less pressure, and thirteen thousand homes that never broke ground.

So this is not lazy councils. It is worse.

**London is building in the wrong places.**

---

**[1:55 — ASK THE ROOM]**

So I built this, so anyone can check their own area. Shout me a borough.

**[CLICK IT ON THE MAP. WAIT.]**

Households. Children. How many were sent out of the borough. What it costs.

**[POINT AT THE EMAIL BUTTON — DO NOT CLICK]**

And a ready-written email to your councillor, with your own numbers already in it.

---

**[2:20 — SCROLL TO "SO BUILD HERE FIRST"]**

And because the homes are in the wrong places, I can say where to start.

Six boroughs, where need is worst **and** permission already exists.

Tower Hamlets. Newham. Enfield. Southwark. Haringey. Wandsworth.

Thirty-seven thousand approved homes, never started. Twenty-four thousand families waiting.

The permission to house all of them, one and a half times over, already exists. Nobody has to
approve anything new.

---

**[2:40 — DO NOT SKIP]**

Two things I could be wrong about.

Unbuilt does not prove land banking — some of it is normal building time.

And my correlation is a null result. With thirty-one boroughs I could only detect a strong link.

---

**[2:50 — LOOK UP AT THE ROOM]**

Every number is open government data. The data to argue for fixing this already existed.
I just made it something you can type your own postcode into.

Thank you.

# Cheat sheet

## Five actions only

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| 0:30 | Scroll to red/green chart. Point. |
| 1:25 | Scroll to scatter. Point at Barnet, then Greenwich. |
| 1:55 | Ask for a borough. Click it **on the map**. |
| 2:05 | Point at the email button. **Do not click.** |
| 2:20 | Scroll to "So build here first". |

## Six numbers to know without looking

- 100,930 children
- £740 million, tripled in three years
- 71% of the homelessness budget
- 112,104 homes never started
- No link between need and failure to build
- 6 boroughs, 37,000 homes already approved

## If something goes wrong

**Wifi dies.** Double-click `homes-vs-hotels.html` on the laptop. Everything works except the
postcode box.

**A borough shows a minus number.** That is real. Say: "that one is actually delivering — it is
working through older permissions."

**You lose your place.** Jump to the six boroughs. That is the ending. Say it and stop.

**Running out of time.** Skip the demo, never the six boroughs. The ending is what people vote on.

## The four questions you will get

**"A null result is not a finding."**
> "It is, when the whole room assumes the opposite. Everyone believes councils cry poverty while
> sitting on permissions. At borough level that is not true. And it changes what you would do —
> target the pipeline, instead of blaming councils."

**"Where do the cost numbers come from?"**
> "Form RO4 of the government's revenue outturn. What each council reported it actually spent.
> I use net, not gross, because housing benefit covers most of the gross figure. Net is what
> lands on the borough's own budget."

**"Isn't the unbuilt gap just normal building time?"**
> "Partly, for the completions gap, and I say so on the page. But a hundred and twelve thousand
> homes never broke ground at all. Building time does not explain a site that never started."

**"Hillingdon at 287% of reserves — is it bankrupt?"**
> "No, and I don't claim that. It pays from revenue, not reserves. It shows how big the bill has
> become next to the money it could reach for in an emergency."
